# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SillyTavern character card authors who edit Chinese character cards, world books, AI presets, regex scripts, and related local writing assets. They use the app as a focused desktop/mobile PWA while maintaining reusable roleplay assets.

## Product Purpose

Space Station is a pure-client editing tool for SillyTavern assets. It helps users import, edit, organize, export, and sync character cards, world books, presets, and notes without requiring a backend. Success means the editor preserves SillyTavern-compatible data reliably while keeping dense editing workflows clear on both PC and mobile.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and polished. The interface should feel like a calm utility console: familiar enough to trust, refined enough to enjoy, and never louder than the user's writing work.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative dashboards, novelty controls, heavy color, and layouts that hide important editing state. Avoid treating special SillyTavern structure fields as ordinary user content when that would corrupt or confuse exports.

## Design Principles

- Keep editing flows dense but legible.
- Preserve SillyTavern data semantics before visual convenience.
- Make mobile layouts structural, not merely compressed desktop layouts.
- Prefer predictable controls, clear labels, and stable insertion behavior.
- Store user preferences in durable app settings when they should survive sessions and sync.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, touch-friendly mobile targets, keyboard-usable controls, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. The interface language is Chinese-only unless a data field itself comes from SillyTavern or an imported file.
